Family

Talking with a parent about care

Start early, listen more than you speak, and let them lead the plan.

Pick a calm moment. Ask what matters most to them. Offer small trials, like one visit a week, before bigger changes.

Safety

Making a home fall-safe

Small changes prevent most fall injuries.

Improve lighting, remove loose rugs, add grab bars in the bathroom, and keep paths clear at night.

Dementia

Daily routines for memory care

Predictable days reduce stress for people with dementia.

Keep meals, walks and rest at the same times. Use simple choices and familiar music.

Nutrition

Easy, nourishing meals for seniors

Simple ideas for appetite, hydration and healthy ageing.

Choose soft, colourful foods, offer small meals often, and keep water within reach.

Mobility

Gentle exercises to stay mobile

Short daily routines that protect balance and strength.

Try seated marches, ankle circles or tai chi, and always check with a nurse first.
